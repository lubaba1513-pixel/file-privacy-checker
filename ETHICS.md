# Responsible use

File Privacy Checker helps reduce accidental disclosure when sharing text. Its purpose is to support careful review of information you own or have permission to handle.

- Respect the privacy and confidentiality obligations attached to your source material.
- Review the full output. An empty findings list is not proof that nothing sensitive remains.
- Use fictional examples in screenshots, issues, pull requests, and demonstrations.
- Remember that unchecked findings are included in the exported text.
- Do not use the tool to conceal falsified evidence, misrepresent records, or bypass lawful accountability.
- If a real credential has already been exposed, redacting a later copy does not revoke it. Follow your organization's credential rotation and incident-handling process.

The tool makes no compliance certification, independent audit, or complete data-loss-prevention claim. It cannot establish consent, authorization, or the sensitivity of every passage.

These are responsible-use guidelines. They do not add restrictions to or replace the [MIT license](LICENSE).
